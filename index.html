Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between porcelain and composite veneers?

Porcelain veneers are custom ceramic shells known for stain resistance and a lifelike shine. Composite veneers are shaped from tooth colored resin in the office and are often more budget friendly. We review both during your consultation so you can choose the option that fits your goals and budget.

How long do dental veneers last?

Longevity depends on the material, your oral hygiene, and daily habits. Porcelain veneers often last a decade or more with good care, while composite veneers may need touch ups sooner. Regular checkups, careful brushing and flossing, and avoiding hard objects help protect your veneers.

Is getting veneers painful?

Most patients find the process comfortable. Some veneer plans involve light reshaping of enamel, and we discuss comfort options before we begin. Mild sensitivity can occur for a short time afterward. Your dentist will explain what to expect so there are no surprises.

Will my smile look natural?

Yes, that is our goal. We match shade, shape, and size to your face, skin tone, and existing teeth, and we preview the plan with you first. A natural result should look like a healthier, brighter version of your own smile.

How do I know if I need a smile makeover?

If you feel self conscious about stains, chips, gaps, uneven edges, or the overall look of your teeth, a smile makeover may help. A consultation is the easiest way to learn which treatments suit you and whether a smaller option could meet your goals.

Can I whiten my teeth before getting veneers?

Often yes, and many patients do. Whitening natural teeth first helps us choose a veneer shade that blends with your smile. Veneers and bonding do not change color with whitening, so planning the order of treatment matters.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and credit cards. During your consultation we outline the expected cost of your plan so you can decide with confidence, and we are happy to discuss how to stage treatment.

What are your office hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. You can call 561-372-6177 or use the contact form to request a visit.
